About

I'm a Software Development Engineer at Visa Inc. in Bengaluru, contributing to Open VisaNet — Visa's next-generation payment processing platform. My work involves designing and building authorization features in Go that achieve functional parity with decades-old mainframe systems. You know that split second between tapping your card and the relieved beep of approval? Yeah — I build that. (And occasionally, the declined one too.)

I graduated from College of Engineering, Guindy (Anna University) in 2024 with a degree in Information Technology and a CGPA of 9.22/10. Outside of production systems, I spend time on applied AI — RAG pipelines, fine-tuning, and tools that reduce the gap between raw LLM capability and real engineering workflows.

I also write occasionally about the things I'm learning — check out the blog.

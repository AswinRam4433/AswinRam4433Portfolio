## The right book at the right time

When I first began my role as a SWE at OVN in Visa, the word "microservices" was being thrown around an awful lot. My knowledge of microservices at the time was very, very surface level. A quick search on the internet led me down a Reddit rabbit hole and almost every post/comment with a decent number of upvotes referred to Sam's book. If you know me, I'm a big fan of books(especially physical copies). A while back, I stumbled upon this social media post that basically said that if you buy a book(even though it might be a tad expensive), the upside is you might have a life changing takeaway and at the absolute worst, you lose a bit of money. Motivated by this theory, I went ahead and purchased this book. And in hindsight, it is probably one of the best decisions I've made wrt books I'd say(can't really think of something that even comes close off the top of my head tbh).

More than the contents of this book, one thing that I absolutely loved about this was the timing. It was very early in my OVN days and I was going through the OVN codebase, the walkthroughs of the major services by the architects, the coding standards etc. And I was very clearly able to relate the best practices outlined in this book with the things we were doing in OVN. It felt absolutely surreal whenever I had a eureka moment about how a concept from the OVN codebase related almost perfectly with something being explained in the book.

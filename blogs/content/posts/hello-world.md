## Why write and why now?

I've been meaning to start writing for a while - my own blog, my thoughts and for some reason I always put it off for later. Until now when a stomach bug forced me out of action and in bed. I seriously began exploring my options for hosting a blog(my only criteria was that the hosting had to be free) and tbh I was spoilt for choice. One of the easiest options(and maybe even the most recommended option) was Github pages. This was quite easy to setup and GitHub took care of all the grunt work for free with one catch - your repo would have to public. I initially tested the waters with this option but for some reason I hated having my blog repo public - this blog was going to be my safe space and to have a stranger be able look into the inner workings and the raw, ugly stuff felt weird. The next natural place I looked at was Vercel. Now Vercel gave me everything I needed - it was free, it let my repo be private, was still somewhat easy to use. All the boxes were ticked and now I began porting my blog from Github pages to Vercel. The other thing was, I didnt wanna write up the content of each blog into an HTML doc. It had to be a markdown file that was picked up by an SSG and turned into a proper webpage with all the bells and whistles and for this, I use [Hugo](https://gohugo.io) with the [Blowfish theme](https://blowfish.page)

Most of what I know came from someone else writing it down. A blog post, a README file, a Wiki at work, a LinkedIn post, a comment block explaining how a piece of code worked(or even memes believe it or not). Writing is how ideas stay alive past the conversation they were born in. This blog is a place for me to keep the chain going. I want to record the stuff that I learnt while I was stuck on something or to show off to the world something cool that I built in the hopes that one day it will help someone(or even myself) who is stuck on something similar find their way through it much faster.

## How is each blog post gonna look like?

Short posts when I have a specific thing to say. Longer ones when a topic earns it like a project walkthrough for example. Code when it helps. Oh and definetely some memes

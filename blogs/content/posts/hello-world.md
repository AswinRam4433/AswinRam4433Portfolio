I've been meaning to start writing for a while. This is that start.

## Why write?

Most of what I know came from someone else writing it down. A blog post at 2am that explained exactly the thing I was stuck on. A README that saved me hours. Writing is how ideas stay alive past the conversation they were born in.

## What I'll write about

Mostly things I'm working on or thinking through — distributed systems, Go, applied AI, and the occasional rabbit hole that doesn't fit a neat category. No editorial calendar. Just things worth saying.

## Format

Short posts when I have a specific thing to say. Longer ones when a topic earns it. Code when it helps. No filler.
